# Research Context and Preliminary Prior-Art Reading

This is a starting reference list, not a complete novelty search, patentability opinion, or freedom-to-operate analysis. The relevant question is how each reference's actual claims and disclosure compare with the *specific technical features* claimed for VitaGuard.

## 1. Patent references to read

### WO2017184702A1 — Worker safety system
https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2017184702A1/en

The published disclosure describes body-worn sensors for worker physiological/behavioral measurements, comparing current measurements with stored or typical sensor data, and alerting a remote location or other workers. Relevant to baseline comparisons, multisensor safety monitoring and local/remote alerts.

### KR20250032156A — Industrial safety management system and method
https://patents.google.com/patent/KR20250032156A/en

The publication describes use of worker biometric information, gas information and impact information with LoRa communication and AI-related hazard/incident analysis in an industrial safety context. Relevant to the broad combination of biometrics, gas, impact/motion and LoRa. Its disclosed arrangement differs in places from the VitaGuard forearm-worn configuration, but it is close enough in subject matter to require careful claim comparison.

### CN116500202A — A wearable wristband gas detection device
https://patents.google.com/patent/CN116500202A/en

The published document describes a wearable gas-detection band with gas sensors, a heart-rate sensor, wireless transmission and GPS positioning. Relevant to wearable integration of gas and physiological sensing.

### WO2010019025A2 — Gas Detection System and Method
https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2010019025A2/en

This is the gas-detection reference supplied by the project team. It describes a gas sensor assembly, receiver and radio-based responses at different detected gas levels. Relevant to sensor/receiver design, threshold levels and alarm signaling.

### US20250349205A1 — Wearable safety monitoring for the hydrocarbon industry
https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250349205A1/en

A further source to review for wearable gas-related worker safety and the hydrocarbon industry.

The team's consolidated report compares studies on:

- optical sensor noise during movement;
- gas/temperature/vital monitoring with ESP32 and cloud services;
- AI-based fall detection using accelerometer/IMU sequences;
- LoRa-based or infrastructure-light worker monitoring;

Confirm that each paper exists and that the described results actually belong to that source.

## 4. How to compare patent claims

Build a feature-by-feature chart for each candidate claim:
- exact feature in VitaGuard;
- where it is supported in the implementation/specification;
- which prior-art document discloses the same feature;
- what technical feature differs;
- evidence supporting any technical effect of the difference.

Do not base novelty arguments solely on market comparisons or a claim that no single product contains every advertised feature. An examiner can consider documents individually and in combination under the applicable law and practice.
